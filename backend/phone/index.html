Tips for a phone used as CCTV
  1. Keep it plugged in and this page open. The screen can stay dark.
  2. If the picture stops when the screen turns off, set the screen timeout to the longest option.
  3. Warnings, the siren and the owner's voice play from this phone's speaker. Turn the volume up.
  4. The microphone lets Guardian notice loud sounds. The owner can listen in; this page shows when they do.
